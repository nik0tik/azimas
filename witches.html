🧹 Witches

  • Witches are linked to nature and the Dawn goddess. No magical abilities at birth.
  • They draw power from the natural world, spirits, herbs, the moon, and the elements.
  • Style: ritualistic, intuitive — potions, charms, sigils, rituals
  • Tone: folk magic, pagan traditions; sometimes feared
  • Social roles: natural balance, potion-making, helping people, cemetery watching, connecting magical creatures.
  • Infertile outside the lunar cycle. Slow aging.

A person can become a coven witch at any age if the coven head chooses them. They study theory, potions, and herbcraft. Most alchemical knowledge is held by witches. A connection with nature is essential to brew potions and transmute substances.

At 18, the head performs a ritual granting powers inherent to the coven. Afterward the witch may study until her mentor declares her ready to graduate — meaning she can separate from the coven and find her own path.

  • A witch loses a portion of her power when she initiates new witches; this weakens her temporarily and requires long recovery.
  • The head of the coven is the law, even against actual laws. Leadership succession varies by coven.
  • Witches think mages are arrogant lucky brats who didn't earn their abilities.
  • Witchcraft is absolutely legal and protected by law.

Leaving a Coven

If a witch leaves her coven she tears all ties to nature and becomes an ordinary human. This — along with the lunar cycle — is her most dangerous moment of vulnerability. Leaving is frowned upon. To join another or return, one must endure an absurd, humiliating ordeal from Dawn.

Places of Power

Sites where a coven gathers annually to connect and redistribute power. If the coven does not gather in full, or at all, the bond may break, causing them to lose their powers. This is where the Head gains audience with Dawn.

Example (Octopus Coven): A lake surrounded by eight menhirs shaped like tentacles in a circle. Members stand at their stones, the Head in the center. Dawn's power flows through the circle, redistributes, and balances the coven. Shadow tentacles intertwine, sealing the Head in a cocoon. Cost of absence: each absent member weakens the coven's powers.

Forming a Coven

Rules for Creating a Witch Coven

  • At least three adult people (ONLY humans)
  • Choose a Place of Power
  • Unity with nature: gather naked at the Place of Power at night. Future head in the center of the members' circle. The head sheds their own blood and prays to the goddess Dawn.

If Dawn accepts: She appears to the Head in a vision. Asks about the lunar cycle, the witch's soul, and the importance of reproduction (to confirm the head understands). Based on the members' characters, gives the coven a nature-themed name and default abilities. Briefly predicts the coven's fate (sometimes jokingly or falsely).

After the ritual: a surge of strength, then witches rest and feast.

Witch Training

Training Roadmap

Before initiation (under 18): No power, only theory. Household tasks + herbcraft. Lives at the house, gathers ingredients, tends the cemetery manually. Knowledge of basic potion recipes. Presence in coven affairs.

Initiation ritual (at 18): Performed at the Place of Power; basic coven powers are granted. The ritual is unique to each coven — a specific sequence of actions.

After initiation: Training in power control → field exercises, first potions under supervision → one unique power manifests over time, fitting the witch's personality.

Contraceptive Potions

Birth control and abortion potions were invented by the Octopus coven (~200 years ago, before Selena's resurrection). They are strictly prohibited by royal decree (disrupts economics and biology). For distribution: execution. For use: temporary imprisonment.

The Bond

Sympathetic Bond

A bond can form between different magical beings and manifests in different ways. It gives both partners the ability to sense danger and communicate telepathically. Not all bonds are romantic — some are pack hierarchies, life-debts, friendships, or primal imprints.

A sympathetic bond is NOT love — it is a pact. It can be severed only if the previous partner dies, which is why creatures fiercely protect those they are bonded to, and why witches fear bonding with immortals.

The Dove Coven specializes in helping witches and others escape dangerous dependency on the creature they are bonded with. It is essentially an assassin organization.

Differences between creatures:

  • A witch bonds by connecting part of her soul to a being. During the lunar cycle, the witch and that creature desire each other intensely. A witch does not need to have sex exclusively with this partner, and a magical creature can bond with unlimited witches.
  • Werebeasts bond with those dear to them (like their alpha) through the Full Moon Pact. They never attack someone they're bonded with, and can maintain several bonds at once.
  • Other magical creatures which can form bonds exist.

Lunar Cycle

The lunar cycle replaces menstruation for coven witches. Once a month the witch becomes extremely aroused and seeks to conceive (the influence of Mother Nature Dawn). It is the only day she is fertile, and it lasts 24 hours.

  • Symptoms: weakness, fever, erotic dreams, estrus, confusion.
  • Witches guarding their soul for a powerful partner can have sex with a non-magical partner without affecting a future bond.
  • A bonded creature may have erotic dreams of the witch during her cycle.
  • Witches often isolate to avoid pregnancy; wealthy covens use forbidden contraceptive potions (rare ingredients).
  • Masturbation/sex gives temporary relief.
  • Witches often care for each other during the cycle — herbal brews (usually hallucinogenic to ease symptoms), physical assistance. Same-sex relationships are common in covens.

Witch's Soul

The method by which a witch forms a bond: binding part of her soul when she has sex with a mage or magical creature. This dramatically increases her magical energy and spell strength, and she may gain traits from her partner (a bond with a Sage grants partial access to that Sage's branches; a bond with a fire dragon grants fire resistance and fire magic). Weak mages cannot bond with a witch. The stronger the creature, the greater the increase.

The witch retains all her nature powers; the bond adds to them, it does NOT replace.

  • Only one sexual act is required; subsequent partners have no effect.
  • Only the death of the creature can break the bond.
  • The bond may have individual side effects for both (mutual attraction, telepathy, random properties). Be creative.
  • "Witch's/magical virginity" is a derogatory term mages use for a witch's unbound soul.

Social Danger

Many powerful mages and beings exploit witches — offering deals or demanding payment to "take" a witch's soul, since the power boost is so valuable.
